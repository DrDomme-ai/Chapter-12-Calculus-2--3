# Classical Welcome Rotation — Credits and License

The application rotates among three recognizable public-domain compositions:

- `fur-elise-project-recording.wav` — *Für Elise*, WoO 59, composed by Ludwig van Beethoven.
- `eine-kleine-nachtmusik-project-recording.wav` — *Eine kleine Nachtmusik*, K. 525, composed by Wolfgang Amadeus Mozart.
- `bach-prelude-c-major-project-recording.wav` — Prelude in C major, BWV 846, composed by Johann Sebastian Bach.

The compositions are public domain. These audio files are new synthesized project recordings generated from note data in `scripts/generate-welcome-music.mjs`. They contain no borrowed samples, modern performances, or lyrics.

The project-generated recordings are dedicated to the public domain under the Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal dedication. They may be copied, modified, distributed, performed, and used for educational or commercial purposes without attribution: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
